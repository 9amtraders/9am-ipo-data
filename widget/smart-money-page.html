Bulk deals and block deals — explained simply

What is a bulk deal?

A bulk deal is when one buyer or seller trades more than 0.5% of a company's total shares in a single day. NSE publishes every bulk deal each evening with the client's name, so you can see which big investor bought or sold.

What is a block deal?

A block deal is a single large trade of at least ₹10 crore, done in a special trading window (8:45–9:00 AM and 2:05–2:20 PM). Block deals are usually negotiated between two big institutions — a fund selling a big stake to another fund, or a promoter selling to an investor.

How does this tracker show "where the money is going"?

For every stock we add up all buy deals and subtract all sell deals. If buyers put in more money than sellers took out, the stock shows net buying (green). The Money River and Bubble Map then show you which stocks and sectors big money is rotating out of and into.

What is "HFT churn" and why hide it?

High-frequency and proprietary trading firms often buy and sell almost the same quantity of a stock on the same day. That shows up as a bulk deal but it is not real investing — they end the day with no position. We detect same-day buy + sell by the same client and hide it by default, so the big numbers reflect real conviction. Turn the toggle off to see everything.

What does "Bought, not sold" show?

It keeps only investors who bought a stock and have no sell deal reported in that same stock during the period you picked. It points to buyers who are holding, not flipping. Sales smaller than the bulk deal limit are not reported, so treat it as a strong hint, not proof.

Can I see an investor's past bulk deals?

Yes. Tap any investor's name to see every bulk and block deal they made in the last 3 months: what they bought, what they sold, their average price, whether they are still holding and how the stock has moved since.

What is an accumulation streak?

When the same big investor buys the same stock on two or more different days, they are building a position step by step. That repeat buying is a stronger signal than a one-time deal, so the tracker flags it with a 🔥 badge.

What does "Since deal" mean?

It is the percentage move from the price in the deal to the stock's latest closing price on NSE. A ✓ means the move went the big investor's way: price up after a buy, or price down after a sell.

Should I buy a stock because a big investor bought it?

No — not by itself. Big investors can be wrong, can sell later, and often buy at prices you cannot get. Use bulk and block deals as a research starting point, then check the business, valuation and chart. This tracker is for learning, not a buy or sell tip.

When is the data updated?

NSE releases bulk and block deal data every trading day in the evening. This page refreshes automatically around 7:45 PM IST, Monday to Friday, and keeps about 3 months (65 trading days) of history. Tap any investor name to see all their bulk and block deals in that time.
